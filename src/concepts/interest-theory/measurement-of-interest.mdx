# Measurement of Interest

The accumulation function \(a(t)\) tells us how much 1 monetary unit invested at time 0 grows to by time \(t\).

The amount function is:

\[
A(t)=P a(t)
\]

Use the interactive visualizer to compare simple and compound interest.

```tsx
<AccumulationFunctionVisualizer
  principal={1000}
  rate={0.05}
  timeHorizon={10}
  selectedTime={10}
  model="both"
/>
```
